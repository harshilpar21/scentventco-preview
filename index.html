Founder Story

Built from struggle, love and a dream to make luxury feel closer.

Jay Vyas did not start Scentvent Co. as just another perfume label. During a difficult season of life, fragrance became one of the things that helped him feel composed, fresh and hopeful. His wife and life partner saw that love and encouraged him to turn it into a brand.

From room sprays created to bring positivity into everyday spaces to premium perfumes designed for confidence, every Scentvent Co. product carries a real emotional journey. Jay studied the market, explored manufacturing in China, reviewed fragrance oils and kept a hands-on eye on the bottle, finish and final feel.

This is why Scentvent Co. is more than fragrance — it is an invitation to feel premium, motivated and remembered.

Talk to Scentvent ↗
What products does Scentvent Co. offer?

Scentvent Co. offers three collections: Luxury Perfumes (eau de parfum, extrait and the Privé Discovery Box), Room Spray (camphor room sprays) and Agarbatti (premium masala agarbatti: Aahuti, Pushpanjali, Samidha and Somdhara, with an Oudh range coming soon).

How can I place an order?

Select a product and choose Buy Now. The website opens WhatsApp with the product name, price and quantity so the order can be confirmed directly.

Do you manufacture fragrance products for brands?

Yes. Brands looking for fragrance manufacturing, private label or product development can submit the manufacturing enquiry form on the Contact page.

Which agarbatti should I choose?

Aahuti is a sacred dhoop offering for daily puja, Pushpanjali is a floral offering, Samidha is an offering of peace and calm, and Somdhara brings a serene, flowing mood. Each is a 100 g box of premium masala agarbatti made without charcoal.
